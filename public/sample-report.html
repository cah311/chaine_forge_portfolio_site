05 · What we ruled out

The recommendations we didn’t make

Six tools got prescribed. Roughly forty didn’t. These four are the ones you’d most likely have been sold by someone else — here’s why they aren’t in section 04, and what would change our mind.

Jobber~$230/mo CAD

The standard answer for a landscaping company your size, and on paper it replaces four of your six pains at once. We left it out because of your own history: a CRM you abandoned after three weeks, email folders that didn’t stick, and one rule stated plainly at the end of our call. A platform migration in your busiest season is the highest-risk thing you could do this year. What would change our mind: you raising it yourself, off-season, with your office lead driving the rollout — then it’s a real conversation and we’d help you scope it.

CompanyCam~$110/mo CAD, 3 seats

The right tool for the photo problem — it files by GPS automatically, so nobody has to remember anything. We put the free album first because a $110/month subscription for a 45-minute-a-week problem is a poor trade while a free option is untested. What would change our mind: if the album is half-empty a month from now, the discipline isn’t there, and CompanyCam removes the need for it. That’s the trigger — not a hunch.

NiceJob / Podium$105–$350/mo CAD

Review automation, and your review gap is real — nineteen against three hundred. But you have never once asked a customer for a review in a systematic way. Paying $105 a month to automate a request you’ve never made is buying the second step first. What would change our mind: a full season of the free ask in your invoices, and a conversion rate worth automating.

Crew scheduling software$70–$210/mo CAD

You mentioned the whiteboard twice and never once complained about it. It works, the crews read it, and it costs nothing. Replacing a working system to make an assessment look thorough is how software gets bought and abandoned. What would change our mind: a third crew, or the day the whiteboard actually fails you.

We take no commission, referral fee, or affiliate revenue from any tool named in this report. That is why three of the six recommendations cost nothing and one was already sitting switched off inside software you own. Our incentive is that you implement, get the hours back, and come to us for the build in section 08 — not that you buy subscriptions.
